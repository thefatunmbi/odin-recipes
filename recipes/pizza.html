Homemade Pizza

Pizza is a popular dish made with a dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings. The dough is made from flour, yeast, water, salt, and olive oil, then left to rise before baking.

It is loved worldwide for its crispy crust, melted cheese, and ability to be customized with toppings like chicken, pepperoni, vegetables, and mushrooms.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Warm water
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Optional toppings (chicken, pepperoni, vegetables)

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, salt, water, and olive oil to form dough.
  2. Let the dough rise until it doubles in size.
  3. Roll out the dough into a flat circle.
  4. Spread tomato sauce over the base.
  5. Add cheese and toppings.
  6. Bake in a hot oven until golden and crispy.
  7. Slice and serve hot.
