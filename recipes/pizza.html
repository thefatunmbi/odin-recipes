Homemade Pizza

Pizza is a popular dish made with a dough base topped with a rich tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings. The base is usually prepared by mixing flour, yeast, water, salt, and olive oil, then kneading it into a soft dough and allowing it to rise. A tomato sauce is prepared using blended tomatoes, onions, garlic, and herbs, which is spread over the rolled dough. It is then topped with cheese, vegetables, meats, and spices before baking in a hot oven.


What makes pizza stand out is its crispy yet soft crust combined with melted cheese and flavorful toppings. It is commonly served at gatherings, restaurants, and celebrations, and can be customized with ingredients like pepperoni, mushrooms, chicken, peppers, onions, and olives. The result is a warm, cheesy, and satisfying meal enjoyed worldwide.


Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Yeast
  • Warm water
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Garlic
  • Onions
  • Oregano
  • Thyme
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni or chicken (optional)
  • Bell peppers (optional)
  • Mushrooms (optional)
  • Olives (optional)


Steps

  1. Prepare the dough: Mix flour, yeast, salt, warm water, and olive oil, then knead until smooth and let it rise.
  2. Prepare the sauce: Cook blended tomatoes, onions, garlic, and herbs until it thickens into a rich sauce.
  3. Roll the dough: Flatten the dough into a circular shape for the pizza base.
  4. Add sauce: Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Add toppings: Sprinkle mozzarella cheese and add toppings like vegetables or meats.
  6. Bake the pizza: Place in a hot oven and bake until the crust is golden and the cheese melts.
  7. Slice and serve: Remove from the oven, slice into pieces, and serve hot.
